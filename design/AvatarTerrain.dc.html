AVATAR TERRAIN · STAGE 2

Walking inside the window

The avatar leaves its 640×200 box and walks the whole window. Terrain comes from the DOM, so the same physics later reads screen rectangles instead — nothing in the avatar changes.

Any element carrying data-terrain contributes a floor at its top edge. Nothing else is special-cased — add the attribute, the avatar can stand on it.

One canvas over the whole window. Clicks pass through to the dashboard; the avatar is hit-tested against its own rect on a window listener, so clicking it still focuses that session.

RECOMPUTE ON LAYOUT CHANGE

A ResizeObserver on the stage plus a scroll listener rebuilds the floor list. Expanding a column moves the ground under the avatar mid‑stride — it falls and re‑settles on its own. That is the intended behaviour, not a bug to suppress.
